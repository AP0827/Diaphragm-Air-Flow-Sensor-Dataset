07 — Key Takeaways

What the Corrected Analysis Says

The new workflow gives a more defensible answer than the earlier Raw/Delta-driven model.

01
The model now answers the right question.

Inputs are external acceleration axes plus temperature; Flow is the target. Raw and Delta are deliberately excluded.

02
There is apparent within-session sensitivity.

Regression finds statistically significant coefficients, with AccZ showing the strongest absolute acceleration coefficient.

03
The effect does not generalize cleanly.

External validation prefers the constant baseline, so acceleration and temperature alone are not enough for reliable Flow prediction across captures.

04
Session shift is likely important.

The external capture has wider AccY and AccZ variation, so coefficients learned from one capture transfer poorly to another.

08 — Limitations & Next Steps

Calibration Strategy

The current evidence is diagnostic, not a final calibration curve.

  • Collect controlled acceleration sweeps: Hold true airflow constant while varying each axis independently.
  • Separate temperature drift: Repeat at stable temperature bands to prevent TempC from absorbing session effects.
  • Use session-aware validation: Keep external captures as the final check before trusting sensitivity coefficients.
  • Report Raw/Delta separately: They are useful diagnostics, but should not be mixed into the acceleration-sensitivity model.
